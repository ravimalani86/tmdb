Privacy Policy

App: Movflik (Android) · Package: com.growdev.movflik

Important: Movflik is a discovery app for movie and TV information and trailers. It does not stream full-length movies or TV shows. Movflik is not affiliated with Netflix, Inc. or any other streaming service whose name or logo may appear as catalog metadata.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Movflik (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using Movflik, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall and stop using the app.

2. What the app does

Movflik lets you:

  • Browse movie and TV catalog information (titles, posters, overviews, genres, providers)
  • Search and filter titles
  • Watch trailers (via YouTube player)
  • Manage personal lists such as My List and Watched
  • Create local profiles on your device (display name / color)

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information stored on your device

Using Android app storage (for example SharedPreferences / local cache), Movflik may store:

  • Profile data — profile display name and color (up to several local profiles)
  • Device / profile identifier — a random UUID generated on your device (not your Google account)
  • Recently viewed titles — a short on-device history
  • Local analytics counters — simple event counts (for example app opens, poster taps, trailer opens). These stay on your device and are not uploaded to an analytics server
  • App cache / settings — cached catalog responses and a copy of remote configuration so the app can load faster and work when offline when possible
  • Ad-related local state — for example temporary “pause ads” reward state when that feature is enabled

3.2 Information sent to our servers

Catalog and list features contact our API (for example hosted at app.myappworld.in). Requests may include:

  • An API key used by the app to access our backend
  • Your random device / profile ID
  • List actions — titles you save or mark as watched, linked to that ID
  • Filter parameters you choose (search text, genre, language, provider, country) needed to return results

We do not require your real name, email address, phone number, or password to use Movflik. There is no traditional account login.

4. How we use information

  • Provide catalog browsing, search, detail pages, and trailers
  • Sync My List / Watched state to your device / profile ID
  • Apply remote configuration (features, ads, force-update, maintenance)
  • Show advertisements when enabled
  • Improve reliability and user experience
  • Respond to support or data-deletion requests you send us

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we process information based on:

  • Performance of the service you request (catalog, lists, trailers)
  • Legitimate interests such as securing the API, preventing abuse, and improving the app
  • Consent where required for personalized advertising (controlled via your device / Google ad settings)

6. Data sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the app:

  • With hosting / infrastructure providers that run our API
  • With Google (AdMob, Firebase, YouTube) as described above
  • If required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer (with notice where required)

7. Data retention

  • On your device: until you clear app storage or uninstall Movflik
  • On our servers: list data tied to your device / profile ID is kept while the feature is used, until you request deletion, or until we remove inactive records during routine maintenance
  • Ads / Firebase / YouTube: retained according to those providers’ policies

8. Children’s privacy

Movflik is intended for a general audience interested in movies and TV and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Movie and TV posters or trailers may include mature themes. Do not use Movflik if you are under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Movflik is not designed for use primarily by children and should not be submitted under Google Play’s Families / Designed for Families programs unless content and ads are fully child-directed compliant.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete local data: Android Settings → Apps → Movflik → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app
  • Remove list items: use in-app My List / Watched controls
  • Advertising ID / personalization: Android Settings → Google → Ads (wording may vary by device) to reset or limit ad personalization
  • Request server data deletion: email us (below) and include your device / profile ID if you know it, or describe your request. We will delete list data associated with that ID where reasonably identifiable

Depending on your region (for example GDPR / similar laws), you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise those rights.

10. Permissions

Movflik requires Internet access to load catalog data, images, configuration, trailers, and ads. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or precise location.

11. Security

We use HTTPS for network requests to our API. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data we handle.

12. International users
